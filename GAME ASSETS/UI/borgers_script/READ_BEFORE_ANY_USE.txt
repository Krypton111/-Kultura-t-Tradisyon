Borgers Script is a relaxed handwritten script with a light, friendly rhythm. It carries a natural flow that feels written in one sitting, without rushing or posing.

The letterforms are smooth and slightly bouncy, with gentle connections and open shapes that keep the text readable. Capitals are expressive without being loud, while the lowercase keeps an even pace across words and lines. The overall impression is casual and personal, like a familiar note or a quick signature.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability. 
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers. 

Borgers Script was drawn with simplicity in mind, focusing on a steady hand and natural movement rather than decoration. The shapes are slightly imperfect on purpose, giving the type a human presence that feels honest and unforced.
